# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone others want to work with. Argue about the work, not about the person
doing it.

## What we expect

- **Technical disagreement is welcome; contempt is not.** "This is wrong, and
  here is the measurement that shows it" is exactly the contribution we want.
  "This is wrong and you clearly have no idea what you are doing" is the same
  point made worse.
- **Say what you actually measured.** Distinguish what you verified from what you
  assume, and mark the difference. Inflated or invented results waste other
  people's time and are the one form of carelessness this project treats as
  serious.
- **Accept correction.** Being wrong in public is the normal cost of working in
  public. Update and move on; nobody is keeping score.
- **Assume competence and good faith.** Contributors here span students and
  professionals, across many languages and time zones. English is a second
  language for many of us. Ask what someone meant before deciding they meant
  something bad.
- **Respect the responsible-use policy.** Do not use issues, pull requests, or
  discussions to solicit or share content that [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md)
  places out of scope, or to ask others to help you attack something you have no
  authorisation to touch.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment in any form; discriminatory or demeaning remarks, including about age,
body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
experience level, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity; sexualised language or advances; personal or political attacks;
deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following; publishing others' private
information without their explicit permission; sustained disruption of
discussion; and encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code comments, and any chat or event where you represent the project —
and to conduct outside those spaces when it affects someone's ability to
participate safely here.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately through the channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Reports are handled confidentially; we will not share your identity with the
person you are reporting without your agreement.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will respond
proportionately: usually a private word and a request to change something, and
where necessary an edit or removal of content, a temporary restriction, or a
permanent ban. We will explain a decision to the people it affects. Maintainers
who do not follow this themselves should expect to be held to it by the others —
authority here is not an exemption.
